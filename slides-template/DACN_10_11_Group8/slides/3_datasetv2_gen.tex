\begin{frame}{Handcrafted Demonstration Example}

\scriptsize

\textbf{Question:}

Who is the spouse of the Green performer?

\vspace{0.15cm}

\textbf{Reasoning:}

To answer this question, we first need to identify who the performer
of Green is. According to the document ``Green (Steve Hillage album),''
Green is an album by the British musician Steve Hillage. Therefore,
Steve Hillage is the performer we are looking for.

\vspace{0.1cm}

Next, we need to determine who Steve Hillage's spouse is. The document
``Miquette Giraudy'' describes Miquette Giraudy as Steve Hillage's
partner. Since this document is a personal biography describing her
professional background and personal association with Steve Hillage,
we interpret ``partner'' in this context as referring to a romantic
or life partner rather than a business or professional partner.
Based on this contextual interpretation, we assume that Miquette
Giraudy is Steve Hillage's spouse. Therefore, the answer is
Miquette Giraudy.

\vspace{0.1cm}

\begin{center}
    \textbf{\texttt{<answer> Miquette Giraudy </answer>}}
\end{center}

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Teacher Instruction Rules}

\small

\begin{block}{Dataset Answer}
    Dataset answers are provided only as supplementary reference
    information. Do not trust them unconditionally or use them as
    definitive evidence. Independently evaluate their correctness
    against the provided passages, prior knowledge, and reasoning.
    Never mention or refer to dataset answers in your reasoning
    or final answer.
\end{block}

\vspace{0.2cm}

\begin{block}{Reasoning Instructions}
    Reasoning instructions are human-provided supplementary guidance,
    not mandatory procedures or authoritative evidence. You may follow,
    adapt, reorder, partially use, deviate from, or reject them as
    appropriate. Independently determine the most appropriate reasoning
    approach. Always prioritize factual accuracy and evidential support
    over adherence to the suggested instructions.
\end{block}

\end{frame}
